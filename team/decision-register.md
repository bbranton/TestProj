# Team decision register

The project manager's record of every Team Lead decision about how the
team works. Each product keeps its own register, such as the Canvas
portal's [Product decisions](/docs/canvas-portal/01-roadmap.md#5-product-decisions)
(Team Lead, 2026-10-27, TD-22). Which register a decision goes in, and
how it's recorded, is in
[Decisions are separate from merging](/team/review-protocol.md#decisions-are-separate-from-merging).
Like the product registers, this is a record, not where the Team Lead is
asked.

- **IDs** are `TD-1`, `TD-2`, ..., in the order they were recorded.
  An ID is never reused. TD-19 to TD-21 were D-10 to D-12 in the roadmap
  before the registers were split.
- **Options** names each option; the one a persona recommended is in
  bold. No bold means there was no recommendation, or none is on record.
- **Status** is **Open** while the Team Lead hasn't answered, and
  **Decided** after. A decision the Team Lead later changes stays, with
  the change and its date added.
- **Where the rule lives:** each decided rule is stated in its home, as
  the [ripple check](/team/review-protocol.md#ripple-check-for-process-rules)
  says. This register records the decision and links to the PR that
  carried it out; it doesn't restate the rule.
- The list starts with #91. Team decisions made before then are in the
  history of the team files and the roadmap's change log.
